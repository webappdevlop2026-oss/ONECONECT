Other Booking Services

Shown on customer home

How a new service reaches the customer

1. You add the name here and switch it on.

2. It appears under “Other Booking Services” on the customer home screen straight away.

3. Vendors offering it can list it, and customers can book it.

Switching it off hides it from customers but keeps the existing bookings and their history intact.
